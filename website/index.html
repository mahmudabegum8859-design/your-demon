// SETUP

From Download to Attack in 4 Steps

No root flashing, no PC required — the entire toolkit is a single portable chroot you select from your storage.

Install APK

Download the APK below, allow "install unknown apps" and install Your Demon on any aarch64 Android device.

adb install app-release.apk

Download Core

Grab the core-aarch64.tar.gz chroot and place it on your device storage — one file, everything included.

core-aarch64.tar.gz (121 MB)

Select in App

Open the onboarding wizard, tap "Select chroot from storage" and pick the tarball. The app extracts and boots it automatically.

bootroot: mounted /proc /sys /dev

Attack

Launch WiFi scans, start an Evil Twin or Captive Portal, capture handshakes or enumerate networks — all from the dashboard.
